Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy explains how Kindmade Studio Ltd ("we", "us") handles personal information when you visit kindmade.co or use our apps, including Rega and Nola. Some apps may have their own privacy notice with more detail. Where they do, that notice applies alongside this one.

Who we are

Kindmade Studio Ltd is the data controller for the personal information described here. We are registered in England and Wales, company number 17496861. Registered office: 18 Philips Drive, Whitefield, Manchester, England, M45 7PY. You can contact us at tom@kindmade.co.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs, such as IP address and browser type, to deliver and protect the site. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

Our apps

Depending on the app and the features you use, we may process:

  • Account details you give us, such as your name or email address.
  • The content you write or say in the app, so the app can respond to you.
  • Purchase and subscription status, which is handled by the app store you use.
  • Basic technical information, such as device type and app version, to keep the app working and fix problems.

Some of this information can relate to your mental health or wellbeing. We treat it as sensitive, use it only to provide the service you asked for, and protect it with appropriate security measures.

Your conversations and AI

Conversations in our apps are not used to train AI models. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Why we use your information

We use personal information to provide and improve our apps, to respond to you, to keep our services secure and to meet legal obligations. Our lawful bases under UK GDPR are performance of a contract, your consent (which you can withdraw at any time), our legitimate interests in running a safe and reliable service, and legal obligation.

Service providers

We use trusted service providers, such as hosting and infrastructure providers, to run our services. They may only process information on our instructions. If information is transferred outside the UK, we make sure appropriate safeguards are in place.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access your personal information, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. To use any of these rights, email tom@kindmade.co. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Not a crisis service

Our apps offer support, not medical care, and they are not an emergency service. If you are in danger or need urgent help, contact your local emergency number.

Children

Our apps are not intended for children under the age set out in each app store listing, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
