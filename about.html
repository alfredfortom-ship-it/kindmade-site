About Kindmade Studio

Kindmade Studio Ltd is an independent app studio registered in England and Wales. We design, build and publish mobile apps for mental health, emotional wellbeing and spiritual growth.

What we do

We make consumer apps for iPhone and Android. Each app does one job well: helping someone talk through a hard moment, wind down before sleep, or read one short, useful idea a day. We handle the whole product ourselves: research, design, engineering, content, customer support and publishing on the App Store and Google Play.

Our products

Nola is an AI emotional support app. People use it to talk through stress, worry and everyday emotional load. Nola keeps replies short, remembers context the user chooses to share, and offers practical tools such as breathing exercises, guided sessions and sleep stories. Website: meetnola.app.

Soulfeed is a daily reading app. It turns ideas from the world's spiritual and philosophical traditions into short, clear chapters that people can read or listen to in a few minutes. Soulfeed is in development.

How we build

Our mental health products are designed together with psychologists and are based on published research. They provide support and self-help tools. They are not a medical service and they do not diagnose or treat any condition. Every app shows clear routes to professional and emergency help.

We collect only the data an app needs to work. Personal conversations are never used to train AI models. Paid features are sold as in-app subscriptions through Apple and Google, with the price and renewal terms shown before purchase.
