We build apps that help people live better lives.

Kindmade Studio designs and publishes mobile apps for mental health, emotional wellbeing and spiritual growth. Small, careful products for the moments when people need support, perspective or a little calm.

About us

Kindmade Studio is a small, independent team of product makers. We started with one belief: the tools people reach for when life feels heavy should be kind in tone, honest about what they are, and easy to use on a hard day.

Today we build in two areas. In mental health, we make conversational support apps developed with psychologists and grounded in clinical research. In spirituality, we turn timeless wisdom into short daily reading people actually finish.

Kindmade Studio Ltd is a private limited company registered in England and Wales.

Inside the apps

How we work

Built with experts. Our mental health products are developed with psychologists and based on published clinical research.

Private by default. Conversations in our apps are never used to train AI models, and we collect only what the product needs to work.

Honest about limits. Our apps offer support, not medical treatment, and point people to professional and emergency help when it is needed.
