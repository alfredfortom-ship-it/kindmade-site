Independent app studio

We make calm, careful apps that help people feel better.

Kindmade Studio is a small, independent studio building apps for mental health, wellness and spirituality. We design for the quiet moments when someone needs support, and we treat every conversation with care.

About the studio

We believe good support should be easy to reach, kind in tone and honest about what it is. Our apps are built together with psychologists, and we keep privacy at the centre of every product decision. Conversations in our apps are never used to train AI models.

Kindmade Studio Ltd is a private limited company registered in England and Wales.
