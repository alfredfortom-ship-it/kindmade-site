Subscriptions and billing

Subscriptions bought in our apps are billed by Apple or Google, not by Kindmade directly.

iPhone and iPad: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. From there you can see, change or cancel a subscription.

Android: open the Google Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.

Refunds are handled by the store you paid through. Apple users can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. Google Play users can follow Google's refund steps. If you are stuck, email us and we will help.

Your account and data

You can ask us to export or delete your account and data at any time. Email support@kindmade.co from the address linked to your account, or use the delete option inside the app where available. Read more in our Privacy Policy.

If you are in crisis

Our apps are not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in danger, call your local emergency number now. In the UK, call 999 or contact Samaritans on 116 123. In the US, call or text 988. Elsewhere, find a local helpline at findahelpline.com.
